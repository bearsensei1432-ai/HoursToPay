About HoursToPay

HoursToPay turns prices into hours of your work, so you can decide whether something is worth it before you check out.

A price tag tells you how much money something costs. It doesn't tell you how much of your life it costs. HoursToPay does. Type a price and your pay, and it shows how many minutes, hours or workdays you would need to work to pay for it.

What you can do with it

  • Enter pay per hour, per day or per month, before or after tax.
  • Price items in a different currency from your pay, using up-to-date exchange rates for 25 currencies.
  • Compare a purchase to everyday things like coffee and meals.
  • Plan how long it will take to save for something.
  • Count monthly and yearly costs, like subscriptions, as hours of work a year.
  • Work out the cost per use, and how many minutes of work each use costs.
  • Share your result as an image or a link. Your pay is never included.
  • Keep a wishlist and see its total cost in workdays.

Where it started

HoursToPay began in the Philippines as a small tool called "Sulit Ba?", which means "Is it worth it?". People found the idea useful wherever they lived, so it grew into a calculator for any country and currency.

Your data stays with you

There are no accounts. Your pay, settings and wishlist are saved only in your own browser. See the privacy policy for details.

Not financial advice

HoursToPay is a thinking tool. It does simple maths with the numbers you enter. It does not give financial, tax or legal advice, and the exchange rates are for estimates only.
