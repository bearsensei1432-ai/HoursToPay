What small daily buys cost you in work hours a year

A drink, a snack or a subscription costs minutes of work, which is why it never feels like much. Repeat it all year and it can quietly add up to weeks of work.

A milk tea habit, in pesos

Say you buy a ₱150 milk tea every workday, and you earn ₱700 a day for 8 hours. That is ₱87.50 an hour.

  • One drink costs about 1 hour 43 minutes of work.
  • Over a year of 260 workdays it costs ₱39,000.
  • That is about 446 hours, or 56 workdays of work.

Fifty-six workdays is more than two and a half months of your working year.

This isn't about cutting everything

Some daily habits are worth every hour. The point is to choose them on purpose. A good test: if you had to work an extra week a year to keep this habit, would you?

  • Keep the ones you look forward to.
  • Cut down the ones you buy out of routine, for example from five days a week to two.
  • Cancel subscriptions you haven't opened in a month.

Turn what you save into something you want

Cutting two ₱150 drinks a week saves ₱15,600 a year. Put that toward something on your wishlist, and use the calculator's savings plan to see the date you can buy it.
