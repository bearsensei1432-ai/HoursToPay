₱25,000 a month is how much per hour?

₱25,000 a month works out to ₱142.05 an hour before tax, based on 22 workdays of 8 hours, which is 176 hours a month.

Compared with minimum wage

That is 1.5 times the Metro Manila (NCR) daily minimum wage for non-agriculture workers of ₱94.38 an hour. See what minimum wage buys.

These are gross figures. To see what an hour is worth after tax, multiply the hourly rate by your take-home percentage. At 80%, that is ₱113.64 an hour. See how to find your take-home percentage.
