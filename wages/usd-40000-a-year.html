$40,000 a year is how much an hour?

$40,000 a year works out to $19.23 an hour before tax, based on 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, which is 2,080 hours a year.

If you take two weeks of unpaid time off a year (2,000 hours), the same salary is $20.00 an hour.
