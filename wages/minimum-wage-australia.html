Minimum wage in Australia: what it buys in work hours

The national minimum wage is A$26.44 an hour, in effect from July 1, 2026. Here is what that pays over a day, a month and a year, and how long it takes to buy everyday things.

What minimum wage pays

PeriodBefore tax
Per hourA$26.44
Per day (7.6 hours)A$200.94
Per week (5 days)A$1,004.72
Per month (22 days)A$4,420.77
Per year (12 months)A$53,049

Good to know

  • The official weekly national minimum wage is A$1,004.90 for a 38-hour week. The hourly rate is rounded, so the weekly figure in the table is a few cents lower.
  • Many workers are covered by an award with a higher minimum rate for their job.
  • This page assumes a 38-hour week, which is 7.6 hours a day over 5 days, before tax.

Sources

Prices and wages checked on October 8, 2026. Prices change, so check the source before you buy.
