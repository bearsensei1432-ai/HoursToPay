Minimum wage in the United Kingdom: what it buys in work hours

The National Living Wage for workers aged 21 and over is £12.71 an hour, in effect from April 1, 2026. Here is what that pays over a day, a month and a year, and how long it takes to buy everyday things.

What minimum wage pays

PeriodBefore tax
Per hour£12.71
Per day (8 hours)£101.68
Per week (5 days)£508.40
Per month (22 days)£2,236.96
Per year (12 months)£26,844

Good to know

  • Workers aged 18 to 20 have a minimum of £10.85 an hour.
  • Workers aged 16 to 17 and apprentices have a minimum of £8.00 an hour.
  • This page assumes 8-hour days and 22 workdays a month, before tax and National Insurance.

Sources

Prices and wages checked on October 8, 2026. Prices change, so check the source before you buy.
