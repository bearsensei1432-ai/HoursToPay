Minimum wage in the United States: what it buys in work hours

The federal minimum wage is $7.25 an hour, in effect from July 24, 2009. Here is what that pays over a day, a month and a year, and how long it takes to buy everyday things.

What minimum wage pays

PeriodBefore tax
Per hour$7.25
Per day (8 hours)$58.00
Per week (5 days)$290.00
Per month (22 days)$1,276.00
Per year (12 months)$15,312

Good to know

  • Many states and cities set a higher minimum wage than the federal one. Where they do, the higher rate applies.
  • The federal rate has not changed since July 24, 2009.
  • This page assumes 8-hour days and 22 workdays a month, before tax.

Sources

Prices and wages checked on October 8, 2026. Prices change, so check the source before you buy.
