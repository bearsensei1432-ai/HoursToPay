Minimum wage in the Philippines: what it buys in work hours

The Metro Manila (NCR) daily minimum wage for non-agriculture workers is ₱755 a day, in effect from September 26, 2026. Here is what that pays over a day, a month and a year, and how long it takes to buy everyday things.

Good to know

  • Agriculture workers in Metro Manila have a lower minimum of ₱718 a day.
  • Every region sets its own minimum wage, and most are lower than Metro Manila's. Check your region on the NWPC website.
  • Minimum wage earners in the Philippines are exempt from income tax, but SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG contributions are still taken from pay.
  • This page assumes 8-hour days and 22 workdays a month. If you work 6 days a week, use 26 days a month.

Sources
