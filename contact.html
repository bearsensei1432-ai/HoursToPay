Found a bug, have an idea, or want to suggest a currency? We'd like to hear from you.

Helpful things to include

  • For a bug: what you entered, what you expected, and what you saw.
  • For a wrong exchange rate: the two currencies and the rate you think is right.
  • For an idea: what you were trying to do when you wished the site did it.

We read every message, but we can't give personal financial advice.
