    How it works

    1. Turn your pay into an hourly rate. Monthly pay is divided by your workdays and hours. Daily pay is divided by your hours.
    2. Use what you keep, if you like. Add your take-home % and the rate uses your pay after tax.
    3. Divide the price by that rate. The answer is how long you work to pay for it.
