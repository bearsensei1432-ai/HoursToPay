Prices in work hours · Philippines

How many hours of work is a PlayStation 5 in the Philippines?

At the Metro Manila minimum wage of ₱755 a day, a PlayStation 5 costs 424.2 hours of work. That is about 53.0 workdays.

PlayStation 5: ₱40,032. PS5 with disc drive, official price from May 1, 2026.

Minimum wage: ₱94.38 an hour (Metro Manila (NCR) daily minimum wage for non-agriculture workers, from September 26, 2026).

The maths

₱40,032 ÷ ₱94.38 an hour = 424.2 hours

With 8-hour workdays, that is 53.0 workdays, or about 2.4 months of full-time work. These figures are before tax.

After tax

Minimum wage earners in the Philippines don't pay income tax, but contributions still come out of pay. If you keep 80% of your pay after deductions, every figure above is 25% higher. At minimum wage that means 530.2 hours instead of 424.2.

Saving up for it

Setting aside 10% of a minimum-wage day (₱75.50) every workday, it takes 530 workdays to save ₱40,032. That is roughly 2.0 years.

Ways to make it cost fewer hours

  • Check whether the games you want are on a console you already own.
  • Wait for a bundle that includes a game you were going to buy anyway.
  • Count the games and subscriptions too. A console is rarely the last thing you pay for.
