Prices in work hours · United States

How many hours of work is an iPhone 17 in the United States?

At the US federal minimum wage of $7.25 an hour, an iPhone 17 costs 128.1 hours of work. That is about 16.0 workdays.

iPhone 17: $929. 256GB, unlocked. Apple shows $899 if you activate it with a US carrier at purchase..

Minimum wage: $7.25 an hour (federal minimum wage, from July 24, 2009).

The maths

$929 ÷ $7.25 an hour = 128.1 hours

With 8-hour workdays, that is 16.0 workdays, or about 0.7 months of full-time work. These figures are before tax.

After tax

If you keep 80% of your pay after deductions, every figure above is 25% higher. At minimum wage that means 160.2 hours instead of 128.1.

Saving up for it

Setting aside 10% of a minimum-wage day ($5.80) every workday, it takes 160 workdays to save $929. That is roughly 0.6 years.

Ways to make it cost fewer hours

  • Buy last year's model. Older models usually get cheaper when a new one comes out, and they keep getting updates for years.
  • Look at certified refurbished phones, which come with a warranty.
  • Trade in or sell your current phone to cut the price.
  • Keep your next phone longer. A phone kept for four years costs half as many hours per year as one kept for two.
