Prices in work hours · Philippines

How many hours of work does Spotify Premium cost in the Philippines?

At the Metro Manila minimum wage of ₱755 a day, Spotify Premium Individual costs 1 hr 47 min of work every month, or 21.5 hours a year.

Spotify Premium Individual: ₱169 a month.

Minimum wage: ₱94.38 an hour (Metro Manila (NCR) daily minimum wage for non-agriculture workers, from September 26, 2026).

Why subscriptions feel cheaper than they are

A monthly price is small enough to ignore, and it keeps going until you stop it. Counted over a year, Spotify Premium Individual is 2.7 workdays at minimum wage. That's worth it if you use it every week, and expensive if you've forgotten about it.

Ways to make it cost fewer hours

  • Look at the Duo or Family plans if someone at home also pays for Spotify.
  • Students can get a discounted plan in many countries.
  • If you only listen at home, the free plan with ads may be enough.
