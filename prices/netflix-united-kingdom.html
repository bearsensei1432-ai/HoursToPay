Prices in work hours · United Kingdom

How many hours of work does Netflix cost in the United Kingdom?

At the UK minimum wage of £12.71 an hour, the Standard plan costs 1 hr 6 min of work every month, or 13.2 hours a year.

Netflix Standard: £13.99 a month.

Minimum wage: £12.71 an hour (National Living Wage for workers aged 21 and over, from April 1, 2026).

Why subscriptions feel cheaper than they are

A monthly price is small enough to ignore, and it keeps going until you stop it. Counted over a year, the Standard plan is 1.7 workdays at minimum wage. That's worth it if you use it every week, and expensive if you've forgotten about it.

Read the subscriptions audit guide to check all of yours at once.

Ways to make it cost fewer hours

  • Switch to a cheaper plan, or one with ads where it's offered, if you mostly watch on one screen.
  • Cancel between shows. You can subscribe for a month, watch what you wanted, and cancel.
  • Check whether your phone or internet plan includes it already.
