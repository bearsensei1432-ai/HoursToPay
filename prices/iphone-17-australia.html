Prices in work hours · Australia

How many hours of work is an iPhone 17 in Australia?

At the Australian minimum wage of A$26.44 an hour, an iPhone 17 costs 58.6 hours of work. That is about 7.7 workdays.

iPhone 17: A$1,549. 256GB.

Minimum wage: A$26.44 an hour (national minimum wage, from July 1, 2026).

The maths

A$1,549 ÷ A$26.44 an hour = 58.6 hours

With 7.6-hour workdays, that is 7.7 workdays, or about 0.4 months of full-time work. These figures are before tax.

After tax

If you keep 80% of your pay after deductions, every figure above is 25% higher. At minimum wage that means 73.2 hours instead of 58.6.

Saving up for it

Setting aside 10% of a minimum-wage day (A$20.09) every workday, it takes 77 workdays to save A$1,549. That is roughly 0.3 years.

Ways to make it cost fewer hours

  • Buy last year's model. Older models usually get cheaper when a new one comes out, and they keep getting updates for years.
  • Look at certified refurbished phones, which come with a warranty.
  • Trade in or sell your current phone to cut the price.
  • Keep your next phone longer. A phone kept for four years costs half as many hours per year as one kept for two.
