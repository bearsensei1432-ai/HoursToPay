Prices in work hours · Philippines

How many hours of work is a MacBook Air in the Philippines?

At the Metro Manila minimum wage of ₱755 a day, a MacBook Air costs 773.4 hours of work. That is about 96.7 workdays.

MacBook Air: ₱72,990. 13-inch MacBook Air with M5, base model with 512GB storage.

Minimum wage: ₱94.38 an hour (Metro Manila (NCR) daily minimum wage for non-agriculture workers, from September 26, 2026).

The maths

₱72,990 ÷ ₱94.38 an hour = 773.4 hours

With 8-hour workdays, that is 96.7 workdays, or about 4.4 months of full-time work. These figures are before tax.

After tax

Minimum wage earners in the Philippines don't pay income tax, but contributions still come out of pay. If you keep 80% of your pay after deductions, every figure above is 25% higher. At minimum wage that means 966.8 hours instead of 773.4.

Saving up for it

Setting aside 10% of a minimum-wage day (₱75.50) every workday, it takes 967 workdays to save ₱72,990. That is roughly 3.7 years.

Ways to make it cost fewer hours

  • Decide what you need it for. For browsing, documents and video calls, a cheaper laptop does the same job.
  • Look at the previous model or a certified refurbished one.
  • Check student and teacher discounts if you qualify.
