Prices in work hours · United Kingdom

How many hours of work is an iPhone 17 in the United Kingdom?

At the UK minimum wage of £12.71 an hour, an iPhone 17 costs 70.7 hours of work. That is about 8.8 workdays.

iPhone 17: £899. 256GB.

Minimum wage: £12.71 an hour (National Living Wage for workers aged 21 and over, from April 1, 2026).

The maths

£899 ÷ £12.71 an hour = 70.7 hours

With 8-hour workdays, that is 8.8 workdays, or about 0.4 months of full-time work. These figures are before tax.

After tax

If you keep 80% of your pay after deductions, every figure above is 25% higher. At minimum wage that means 88.4 hours instead of 70.7.

Saving up for it

Setting aside 10% of a minimum-wage day (£10.17) every workday, it takes 88 workdays to save £899. That is roughly 0.3 years.

Ways to make it cost fewer hours

  • Buy last year's model. Older models usually get cheaper when a new one comes out, and they keep getting updates for years.
  • Look at certified refurbished phones, which come with a warranty.
  • Trade in or sell your current phone to cut the price.
  • Keep your next phone longer. A phone kept for four years costs half as many hours per year as one kept for two.
